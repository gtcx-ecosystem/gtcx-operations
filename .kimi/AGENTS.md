# Kimi Agent Protocol — gtcx-operations

> Corporate operations repo. Contains legal, HR, finance, IP, fundraising, CRM, email, and ecosystem health tooling.

## Scope

- Legal contracts and templates
- Budget tracking and financial records
- IP and trademark management
- Ecosystem health monitoring
- SOC 2 and SLSA compliance tooling
- Cross-channel orchestration (email, WhatsApp, calendar)

## Conventions

- Scripts use `tsx` (TypeScript execution)
- Output paths: `workstream/` for reports, `legal/` for contracts
- Country-agnostic language in all strategy docs
- AGPL-3.0 licensed
